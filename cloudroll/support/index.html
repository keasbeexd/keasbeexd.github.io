Cloudroll support

Questions, bug reports and ideas are welcome.

Get help

  • Report a bug or ask a question: open an issue on GitHub. A free GitHub account is needed.

Reporting a bug

It helps to include:

  • Your Cloudroll version (Settings > Version) and iOS version
  • Your Nextcloud and Memories versions
  • What you did, what you expected and what happened
  • The diagnostics log, if the problem is a page that won't load or an error: open Cloudroll's settings (the gear on the timeline, or touch and hold the tab bar), choose Diagnostics and share it. It contains paths on your server, never passwords. Look it over before posting it publicly.

Common questions

Sign-in doesn't finish

Cloudroll uses Nextcloud's own sign-in page, so your password never goes through the app. If the page stays open after you approve access, close it and try again, and check that your server address starts with https://.

Some features are missing

The native viewer, phone photos in the timeline and the native controls need Memories 9 or later, which needs Nextcloud 34 or later. With older versions you get Memories' web interface.

Signing out

Open Cloudroll's settings, swipe left on the account and choose Sign Out. Cloudroll revokes its app password on your server; if it can't reach the server, remove the device under Personal settings > Security in Nextcloud.
