Cloudroll

Your Nextcloud photos on your iPhone, with the feel of a native app.

Cloudroll is an iPhone app for Memories, the photo gallery for Nextcloud. It's free, open source and collects no data. Every feature is included.

Cloudroll is in testing and coming to the App Store. Follow along on GitHub.

What it does

  • Your iPhone's photos in the timeline next to the ones on Nextcloud, with upload, delete (from the phone, Nextcloud or both), share and Save to Photos
  • A native photo viewer with Live Photos, an info panel and smooth swiping
  • Edit photos on your phone; the Photos app can still revert the edits
  • Face ID lock and alternate app icons
  • Several Nextcloud accounts, on one server or several

Requirements

  • iPhone with iOS 17 or later
  • A Nextcloud server with Memories. The native features need Memories 9 or later (Nextcloud 34 or later); with older versions Cloudroll shows Memories' web interface.
